# Peligood Bid Radar regression guardrails

Last updated: 2026-09-22

## Search coverage
- Do not reduce screening to literal VR/AR/metaverse words.
- Read task/detail/RFP clues for content, simulation, digital twin, virtual lab, AI media, education, exhibition, 3D and adjacent work.
- Non-direct candidates may be retained when consortium/partner participation is plausible, but their fit reason must say why.

## URL/provenance
- Every actionable `bid` or `pre-spec` research item must have a non-empty HTTPS provenance URL.
- Prefer verified official notice links.
- If official detail cannot be verified, use the actual discovery/detail page used in research.
- Never fabricate an official URL.
- A `watch` pattern without a current notice cannot be presented as an active bid.

## Lifecycle/deadline
- Keep `bid`, `pre-spec`, `watch`, and `plan` distinct.
- Pre-spec opinion deadline is not a bid deadline.
- Historical/closed items must not appear as active solely because they are relevant.
- Notice number and source remain attached to the record when known.

## UI
- Keep "입찰할 수 있는 공고" as the broad product concept.
- Default list remains deadline-oriented.
- Mobile filters/sort remain usable without horizontal page overflow.
- Official vs discovery provenance must be distinguishable.

## Data integrity
- Do not invent budget, qualification, consortium allowance, deadline or fit evidence.
- Preserve daily research snapshots so later decisions can be audited.
- URL/lifecycle/source contract validation must pass before publication.
